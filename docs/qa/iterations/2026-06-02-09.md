## Identified Defects (Phases 1 Hard Cap)

### Defect 1: Missing Backend Role-Gating for Trip Locking
- **Symptom:** Accountant (`anh`) clicked "Khóa chuyến" and the trip successfully transitioned to `LOCKED` status and posted ledger entries.
- **Root Cause:** `transitionTripStatus` in `backend/src/services/trip.service.ts` (lines 496-520) does not verify that `userRole === Role.ADMIN` or `Role.MANAGER` when transitioning to `LOCKED` (unlike `IN_TRANSIT` and `CANCELED` which correctly check this).
- **Severity:** High (violates key business invariant: only MANAGER/ADMIN can close a trip and trigger ledger postings).

### Defect 2: UI Action Buttons Visible to Unauthorized Roles
- **Symptom:** Accountant (`anh`) sees active, clickable buttons for "Xuất phát", "Khóa chuyến", and "Hủy chuyến" on the trip detail page, even though they lack permission (or should lack permission). Clicking them results in confusing backend 403 errors (or in the case of lock, successfully bypassing the rules).
- **Root Cause:** `frontend/src/pages/TripDetailPage.tsx` computes button visibility (`canDispatch`, `canLock`, `canCancel`) purely on the trip status, without checking the authenticated user's role.
- **Severity:** Medium (poor UX/UI security posture).

---

## Action Plan (Phase 2)
1. **Backend:** Add a role check to the `LOCKED` branch in `backend/src/services/trip.service.ts` to restrict the action to `ADMIN` and `MANAGER`.
2. **Frontend:** Import and use `useAuth` in `frontend/src/pages/TripDetailPage.tsx` to conditionally render or disable these state-transition buttons for unauthorized roles (like `ACCOUNTANT` and `DRIVER`).
