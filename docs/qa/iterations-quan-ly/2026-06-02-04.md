### M4.2 — Note

The customer form does **not** reveal additional dispatch fields when `isCarrier` flips on — by design, the carrier-only fields (`externalCarrierId` selector, freight cost, plate, etc.) live on the **trip form**, not the customer form. The customer-side toggle is a binary marker that simply enables the customer to appear in the carrier dropdown on the trip form (see M5.2). Marking M4.2 as Passed-by-design rather than a literal "shows new fields" test.

### M5.3 — observation (not a bug)
The four required external fields (`externalCarrierId`, `externalFreightCost`, `externalPlateNumber`, `externalDriverName`, `externalDriverPhone`) do not carry the HTML `required` attribute on the inputs; required-ness is enforced at the **Zod layer** on submit (covered in SCC-F2.5, with proper Vietnamese error messages). UI users see backend 400 errors rather than browser-native required hints. Acceptable; recommend a future polish ticket to add `required` for nicer UX.
